Xtranet Technologies IPO is a book build issue of ₹166.80 crores. The issue is entirely a fresh issue of 1.31 crore shares of ₹166.80 crore.

About Xtranet Technologies Ltd.

Incorporated in 2002, Xtranet Technologies is an integrated IT solutions provider offering end-to-end services such as enterprise applications, digital transformation, managed services, proprietary platforms, and strategic technology partnerships. The company serves customers across industries.

Its services are delivered through a mix of onsite and offshore models, supported by subsidiaries, joint ventures, and in-house platforms.

Business Offerings:

  1. Enterprise Applications: ERP Implementation and support services, IT System Integration Services i.e., Network and Security Solution, Virtualization and Cloud Integration, Infrastructure Management Services, etc., Data Centres managment, Application Development & maintenance, etc.
  2. Managed Services: Infrastructure management services covering the design, establishment, and modernization of IT infrastructure.
  3. Digital Services: Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS), Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)
  4. Proprietary platforms: Synergy low-code digital transformation, XtraTrust platform

It derives revenue from fixed-price projects, time-and-materials contracts, and recurring service agreements, with a significant share from Government and PSU clients.

As on April 30, 2026, the company employed 504 permanent employees.

Competitive Strengths

  • Proven track record in executing projects for Government and PSU clients
  • Long standing relationship with marquee customer base
  • Experienced Management Team
  • Geographic Presence and Multi-Location Operations
