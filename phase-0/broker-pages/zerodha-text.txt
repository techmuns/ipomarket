About NFP Sampoorna Foods

NFP Sampoorna Foods Limited is a food processing and trading company engaged in the procurement, import, processing, grading, packaging, marketing, and distribution of dry fruits. The company’s product portfolio includes cashew nuts, makhana (fox nuts), almonds, and walnuts, which are sold through B2B, B2C, institutional, and government channels. It sources raw cashew nuts directly from selected farms in African countries and processes them in-house into different grades of cashew kernels for sale in domestic and regional markets. To expand its offerings in the health-focused food segment, the company added makhana in 2024, almonds in 2025, and walnuts in 2025. Its revenue is primarily generated through the sale and distribution of these products across different customer segments. The company also acquired Yashvardhan Food Industries Private Limited in 2025 through a share swap arrangement.
