About Poojaa Precision Engg.Ltd.

Incorporated in August 1992, Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited is a precision engineering company engaged in the manufacturing of aluminium die-casting and precision-machined components that are used in engines, drivetrains, EV Powertrains, and electrical systems. The company manufactures 600+ SKUs, including safety-critical components, through integrated capabilities in gravity die casting (GDC), low-pressure die casting (LPDC), high-pressure die casting (HPDC), machining, assembly, and engineering services. Its product portfolio caters to industries such as automotive (Commercial Vehicles, Passenger Vehicles, 2 Wheeler, Electric Vehicles), agriculture, defence, energy, healthcare, aerospace, and engineering goods.

The company operates advanced manufacturing facilities equipped with melting units, casting lines, machining centers, and quality assurance infrastructure, enabling end-to-end solutions from product design and engineering to casting, machining, assembly, and finishing. It serves a diversified customer base comprising OEMs, Tier-1 automotive suppliers, and international customers across Germany, the United States, Italy, and Switzerland, while recently expanding into the aerospace sector through approved vendor partnerships.

Poojaa Precision Engg. focuses on precision manufacturing, continuous product development, quality excellence, and long-term customer relationships.

As of March 31, 2026, the company employed 216 permanent personnel across manufacturing, quality assurance, design, maintenance, business development, finance, HR, and other operational functions.

Strengths

  • Integrated aluminium die-casting, machining, and precision engineering capabilities.
  • Comprehensive product portfolio serving automotive, EV, aerospace, and industrial sectors.
  • Diversified domestic and international customer base with long-standing relationships.
  • Strong focus on quality, engineering excellence, innovation, and experienced management.
