    About Vegorama Punjabi Angithi Limited

    Incorporated in 2022, Vegorama Punjabi Angithi has grown from a cloud kitchen and takeaway to a brand that delivers premium-quality vegetarian food of North Indian cuisine and others. The company currently runs its business through 27 cloud kitchens and 2 fine dining restaurants in five cities – Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, and Dehradun. The company operates a chain of restaurants under the "Punjabi Angithi" brand.
    The company offers five types of food brands, including Punjabi Angithi, Chinese Veg Crunch, Dilli Tawa Parantha, Food of Dreams, and Swaad of Punjab. The food varieties offered by the company include North Indian vegetarian cuisine, Indo-Chinese cuisine, Tandoori dishes, Momos, and Delhi-style parantha. As of Dec 2025, E-commerce portals contribute 91.93% to the corporation’s sales, with takeaway services accounting for 4.24%, dine-in for 2.14%, and catering for 1.70%.
    Delhi contributed the highest to revenue at 52.62% in December 2025, followed by Haryana at 24.21%, Uttar Pradesh at 19.26% and Uttarakhand at 3.91%. Delhi’s contribution increased from 43.35% in FY24 owing to an increase in outlets and fine dining. Top 10 customers contributed 92.85% of revenue in December 2025, with the top 2 food delivery portals contributing 51.42% and 40.45%, respectively.

    How to invest in the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    Investors can apply for the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO through their Demat account via the stock exchange or through their broker.

    What is the issue size of Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    What is 'pre-apply' for Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    Pre-applying for an IPO allows you to submit your application before the official subscription period begins.

    Which exchanges will Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO shares list on?

    The IPO shares will typically list on major stock exchanges such as the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), as specified in the IPO prospectus.

    What are the open and close dates of the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO will be open between 2026-05-20 and 2026-05-22.

    What is the allotment date for the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO allotment date is 2026-05-26. For Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO allotment status check, you can visit the website of the registrar of the IPO.

    Who is the registrar of Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    For Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO, the IPO registrar is BIG SHARE SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED . You can check Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO allotment status on the website of the registrar.

    Where is the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO getting listed?

    The shares are proposed to be listed on the BSE and NSE.

    When will Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO shares be credited to your demat account?

    The Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO shares will typically be credited to your demat account one working day before the IPO's listing date, provided your application is successfully allotted.

    What is the lot size and minimum order quantity of the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?

    What is the price band for the Vegorama Punjabi Angithi IPO ?
