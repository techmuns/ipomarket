Shah Investor's Home IPO Details

Bookbuilding IPO | ₹90 Cr | Listing at BSE, NSE | Mon, Sep 28, 2026 - Wed, Sep 30, 2026

Shah Investor's Home IPO is a book build issue of ₹90.17 crores. The issue is entirely a fresh issue of 53.99 lakh shares of ₹90.17 crore.

Shah Investor's Home IPO opens for subscription on Sep 28, 2026 and closes on Sep 30, 2026. The allotment for the Shah Investor's Home IPO is expected to be finalized on Oct 1, 2026. Shah Investor's Home IPO will list on NSE and BSE with a tentative listing date fixed as Oct 6, 2026.

Shah Investor's Home IPO is set issue price band at ₹159 to ₹167 per share. The lot size for an application is 85 shares. The minimum amount of investment required by an individual investor (retail) is ₹14,195 (85 shares) (based on upper price).

Beeline Capital Advisors Pvt.Ltd. is the book running lead manager and MUFG Intime India Pvt.Ltd. is the registrar of the issue.

Refer to Shah Investor's Home IPO RHP for detailed Information.
